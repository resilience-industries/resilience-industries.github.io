VPAT® & Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR) Authoring

A Voluntary Product Accessibility Template (VPAT®) is the standard document produced by vendors to detail how software, websites, and cloud products comply with federal accessibility mandates. Once evaluated by an accredited auditor, it serves as an official Accessibility Conformance Report (ACR).

Government procurement bodies, universities, and enterprise buyers mandate a valid, legally defensible ACR before approving vendor RFPs. Resilience Industries Limited authors comprehensive VPATs backed by screen reader recordings and manual testing governed by certified auditor Nitesh Kumar.

Supported VPAT 2.5 Regulatory Editions

1. VPAT Section 508 (United States Federal)

For vendors bidding on U.S. Federal Government contracts under Section 508. Covers Chapter 3 (Functional Performance Criteria), Chapter 5 (Software), and Chapter 6 (Support Documentation).

2. VPAT WCAG (Web Content Accessibility Guidelines)

Maps conformance exclusively against W3C WCAG 2.1 and WCAG 2.2 across Level A, Level AA, and Level AAA. Required by educational institutions and state government agencies.

3. VPAT EU (European Standard EN 301 549)

Mandated for public procurement within European Union member states under the European Accessibility Act (EAA) and EN 301 549 specifications.

4. VPAT INT (International Unified Edition)

The comprehensive dossier combining WCAG 2.2, Section 508, and EN 301 549. Ideal for global enterprise SaaS platforms operating across multi-jurisdictional procurement pipelines.

Official Conformance Terminology

  • Supports: The product functionality has at least one method that meets the criterion without known defects.
  • Partially Supports: Some functionality does not meet the criterion, but primary workflows remain partially operable.
  • Does Not Support: The majority of platform workflows fail the criterion, creating complete accessibility blockers.
  • Not Applicable: The criterion is not relevant to the platform (e.g., captions on platforms with no multimedia).
