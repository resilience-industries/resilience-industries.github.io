Privacy & Data Protection Policy

Resilience Industries Limited (RIL) operates as an enterprise digital accessibility consultancy and testing laboratory. We treat information security, source code confidentiality, and personal data privacy with the highest degree of professional responsibility.

This Privacy Policy explains how our laboratory collects, processes, and protects your information when you engage our consulting services, access client portals, commission accessibility audits, or make transactions. Our practices strictly align with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP), the European Union's General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and global security standards.

1. Enterprise Confidentiality & Code Non-Disclosure

In the course of accessibility audits and VPAT authoring, clients frequently provide our laboratory with access to pre-production software, proprietary design tokens, and internal staging environments. We maintain strict protocols to safeguard this data:

  • Bilateral NDA Protection: All proprietary codebases, wireframes, and test credentials shared with our laboratory are governed by attorney-drafted Non-Disclosure Agreements.
  • No Commercial Data Exploitation: We never sell, lease, monetize, or publicly disclose client vulnerabilities, accessibility audit dossiers, or architectural flaws.
  • Secure Disposal: Temporary testing credentials and sandbox database accounts are permanently purged from our hardware laboratory within 14 days of final deliverable transmission.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the minimum data required to establish authentic business communications and process consulting transactions:

A. Account & Contact Credentials

Full name, corporate email address, direct phone number, country of residence, and target website URLs submitted through our contact and registration forms.

B. Authentication & Security Tokens

Secure cryptographic identifiers, password hashes, and email verification states managed via Google Firebase Authentication. We never store plain-text passwords.

C. Payment & Transaction Metadata

Transaction reference numbers, amounts paid, and currency identifiers generated during booking checkouts. Payment card numbers, CVVs, and banking PINs are processed directly by Razorpay and never touch our servers.

3. Authorized Third-Party Processors

To maintain defense-grade portal infrastructure, we partner exclusively with verified industry infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase (Google LLC): Facilitates enterprise client identity management, encrypted OAuth handshakes, and password resets over HTTPS/TLS 1.3.
  • Razorpay Software Private Limited: Our authorized payment processing gateway conforming strictly to PCI-DSS Level 1 certification and RBI digital tokenization rules.
  • Web3Forms API: Dispatches encrypted transactional dossiers and technical service inquiries directly to our administrative desk.

4. Zero Invasive Trackers & Cookie Transparency

As advocates for digital integrity, our website employs zero invasive tracking pixels, zero behavioral advertising cookies, and zero social media tracking scripts.

We utilize minimal, privacy-friendly browser localStorage purely to remember your accessible preferences (Dark Mode, High Contrast Mode, Font Scale) and maintain aggregate pageview statistics. This information never leaves your client device.

5. Your Statutory Data Rights

Regardless of your geographical location, Resilience Industries Limited accords all users full statutory data sovereignty:

  • Right to Permanent Deletion: You may permanently purge your user profile, session data, and database records at any time using the "Delete Account" control inside our portal.
  • Right to Rectification: You may update or correct registered email credentials or contact information by submitting a verification request.
  • Right to Data Portability: You may request an export of all formal audit artifacts, issue logs, and receipts associated with your account.
