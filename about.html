Our Auditing Laboratory Methodology

Resilience Industries executes assessments through a rigorous three-tiered framework:

  • Empirical Evidence Logging: Every accessibility violation is documented with time-stamped video/audio captures demonstrating TalkBack or NVDA focus failures.
  • Direct Remediation Patches: Rather than issuing generic recommendations, we deliver functional semantic HTML5 code snippets and validated WAI-ARIA 1.2 state configurations.
  • Statutory Alignment: Deliverables conform to W3C WCAG 2.2 Level AAA, Section 508 of the U.S. Rehabilitation Act, and European Standard EN 301 549 specifications.
