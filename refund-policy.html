Statutory Refund, Cancellation & Fee Settlement Policy

Resilience Industries adheres to principles of commercial integrity and statutory fairness. As a registered enterprise under the Ministry of Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises, Government of India (Udyam Registration: UDYAM-BR-26-0255682), we formulate our cancellation and refund protocols in alignment with the Consumer Protection Act, 2019 and prevailing digital commerce standards[span_2](start_span)[span_2](end_span).

This policy governs commercial terms regarding digital accessibility audits, VPAT ACR authoring, front-end remediation engineering, and training masterclasses.

Service-Specific Cancellation & Refund Rules

1. Accessibility Audits & Diagnostic Health Checks

For Baseline Audits (₹500) and Standard WCAG 2.2 AAA Audits (₹4,999):

  • Prior to Audit Initiation: If a client cancels within 12 hours of booking and prior to the initiation of manual testing in our laboratory, a 100% full refund is issued immediately.
  • Active Laboratory Phase: Once our certified engineers initiate exploratory screen reader testing and logging, labor costs are committed. In this stage, cancellations are eligible for a 50% partial settlement.
  • Post-Delivery: Once final audit evaluation rubrics, video proofs, and code patches are delivered, fees are deemed non-refundable. We provide a 7-day complimentary review window to clarify any technical finding.

2. Enterprise VPAT® 2.5 / ACR Authoring Engagements

Federal procurement VPAT engagements operate on milestone escrow structures:

  • Cancellations requested before staging access credentials are authenticated are eligible for a 90% refund (10% retained for administrative onboarding).
  • If an engagement is terminated mid-cycle by the client, billing is reconciled solely for criteria evaluated up to the formal cancellation date.

3. Training Masterclasses & CPACC Cohorts

For Academy masterclass seats:

  • Cancellations submitted up to 48 hours prior to the scheduled cohort kickoff are eligible for a 100% full refund or a free transfer to a future batch.
  • Cancellations within 48 hours of session start are eligible for a 50% refund or full rollover credit.

4. Refund Settlement Method & Timeline

All approved refunds are settled directly to the original payment instrument (bank account, UPI, or credit card) via our payment gateway partner, Razorpay. Domestic settlements credit within 5–7 business days. International card settlements credit within 7–14 business days depending on the acquiring bank.
