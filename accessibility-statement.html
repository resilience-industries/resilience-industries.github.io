Official Accessibility Statement & Statutory Conformance Declaration

Resilience Industries is fundamentally dedicated to universal digital inclusion. As an accessibility consultancy directed by a 100% blind accessibility specialist, Nitesh Kumar, CPACC, we hold our own public platforms to the highest possible bar of technical compliance.

This web portal has been designed, coded, and verified to achieve 100% full conformance with W3C Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 Level AAA, Section 508 of the U.S. Rehabilitation Act, and European Standard EN 301 549 specifications.

Structural Implementation Highlights

1. Semantic Architecture & Keyboard Navigability

The site utilizes authentic HTML5 landmarks (<header role="banner">, <nav role="navigation">, <main role="main">, <aside role="complementary">, and <footer role="contentinfo">). Visible keyboard focus outlines are persistently maintained with high-contrast rings (WCAG SC 2.4.7), focus is never trapped inside any dialog (SC 2.1.2), and skip-navigation links allow immediate bypass of recurring headers (SC 2.4.1).

2. Enhanced Contrast & Dynamic Visual Controls (Level AAA)

All body copy exceeds the enhanced contrast requirement of 7:1 against adjacent backgrounds (WCAG SC 1.4.6). Large headings exceed 4.5:1. Users can dynamically switch to high-contrast monochrome mode, dark mode, or increase font sizing without text clipping or loss of functionality (SC 1.4.4 & SC 1.4.10 Reflow up to 400%).

3. Assistive Technology Testing Fleet Validation

This platform is verified under native hardware test environments:

  • Google TalkBack 14+ on Android 14 with gesture navigation.
  • NVDA (NonVisual Desktop Access) 2024.x on Mozilla Firefox and Google Chrome.
  • Freedom Scientific JAWS 2024 on Microsoft Edge.
  • Physical keyboard-only navigation without mouse or trackpad emulation.

4. Cognitive Accessibility & Motion Controls

Zero blinking, flashing, or strobe elements exist on this platform (WCAG SC 2.3.1). A persistent "Pause Motion" control immediately freezes live metrics, transitions, and timers to accommodate vestibular motion disorders (WCAG SC 2.2.2). Form errors provide descriptive text instructions (SC 3.3.1 & SC 3.3.3).
