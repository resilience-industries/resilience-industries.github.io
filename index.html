Certified Digital Accessibility Engineering & Compliance

Resilience Industries delivers premier web accessibility testing, manual assistive technology audits, and VPAT / ACR authoring conforming strictly to W3C WCAG 2.2 (A, AA, AAA), Section 508, and EN 301 549 standards.

Directed by Nitesh Kumar, an IAAP-certified (CPACC) visually impaired accessibility specialist (UDID: DL8390220020008444), our engineering laboratory combines automated code validation pipelines with rigorous native screen reader audits across Google TalkBack, Windows NVDA, and Freedom Scientific JAWS to eliminate all digital barriers.

Core Competencies & Services

WCAG 2.2 AAA Audits

Manual screen reader exploratory analysis, focus order verification, keyboard trapping analysis, and contrast testing.

VPAT & ACR Production

Authoring official Voluntary Product Accessibility Templates (VPAT 2.5) conforming to federal and international procurement rules.

Remediation Engineering

Direct front-end code patches, semantic HTML refactoring, and accessible design system architecture for engineering teams.
