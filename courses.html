Professional Accessibility Training & Certification Masterclasses

Digital accessibility is not merely about memorizing guidelines—it demands an authentic understanding of how assistive technologies interpret code structures. The training academy at Resilience Industries bridges theoretical compliance with empirical engineering practices.

Led directly by Nitesh Kumar, CPACC, each curriculum provides hands-on exposure to native screen readers (Google TalkBack, Windows NVDA, and Freedom Scientific JAWS), real-world WAI-ARIA debugging, and defense-grade documentation authoring.

Official Masterclass & Certification Tracks

1. IAAP CPACC Exam Preparation Bootcamp

₹2,999 / $40

A comprehensive cohort designed to master the three core domains of the IAAP Certified Professional in Accessibility Core Competencies (CPACC) credential:

  • Disabilities, Challenges & Assistive Technologies (Sensory, Physical, Cognitive, Multiple).
  • Accessibility and Universal Design (Universal Design principles, architectural concepts).
  • Declarations, Laws, and Management Strategies (ADA, Section 508, EN 301 549, CRPD).

2. Native Screen Reader Engineering & Auditing Workshop

₹1,999 / $25

Taught from the perspective of an authentic non-visual operator. Learn how to configure, operate, and audit digital interfaces using:

  • Google TalkBack: Android swipe exploration, gesture traps, dynamic content live regions.
  • NVDA & JAWS: Windows desktop virtual cursor navigation, table markup, keyboard focus traps.
  • Evidence Recording: Capturing synchronized speech synthesizer audio logs for developer reporting.

3. WAI-ARIA 1.2 & Semantic HTML5 Deep Dive for Developers

₹2,499 / $35

Engineered specifically for front-end developers, UI/UX engineers, and QA automation teams:

  • The 5 Rules of ARIA and why "No ARIA is better than Bad ARIA".
  • Building accessible modal dialogs, tab panels, accordions, and custom dropdown menus.
  • Mastering aria-live polite versus assertive regions and accessible name computation.
