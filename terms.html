1. Scope of Accessibility Consulting Services

Resilience Industries Limited delivers specialized digital accessibility evaluations, regulatory authoring, and assistive technology testing. All consulting deliverables are governed by the following operating protocols:

  • Compliance Standards: Audits evaluate digital assets against W3C WCAG 2.2 (Level A, AA, and AAA), Section 508 of the U.S. Rehabilitation Act, and European Standard EN 301 549 specifications.
  • Empirical Assistive Tech Testing: All evaluations are personally conducted or supervised by certified specialist Nitesh Kumar, utilizing physical hardware keyboards and real screen readers (Android TalkBack, Windows NVDA, and JAWS).
  • SLA & Response Protocol: Initial formal communication and technical scoping commence within 24 to 72 business hours following payment confirmation.
